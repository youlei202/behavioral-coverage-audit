# Known scientific and reproduction limits

- The balanced reference-answer scalar audit uses cyclic option rotations, two endpoints, matched MSE/MAE fitting, and common-question bootstrap intervals. Canonical scalar trajectories use five doses and trackwise evaluation. The complete-option diagnostic uses canonical order, five doses, vector squared-loss fitting, and TV-selected single peers. Transferred scalar weights and refitted vector weights remain separate. Bootstrap intervals are not transferred between these designs.
- Ten fixed question splits overlap; they are not ten independent datasets. Pointwise intervals and the historical claim gates are preserved. Generation diagnostics use a permissive answer parser and remain auxiliary, not verified open-ended-answer accuracy.
- Historical V2.3 Stage C initially failed 19 numerical gates. The archived final package includes its later authorized simplex-feasibility repair and successful completion; this task did not rerun the optimization or silently introduce a new repair. Original failure and repair records remain available.
- Vision residual arrays preserve all 400 evaluation positions per context. Geometry NPZ files retain coordinates, fit IDs, hull vertices, weights and explained variance for six separately fitted PCA views. They do **not** contain the high-dimensional response vectors needed to independently refit PCA. Coordinate replay is verified; a fresh PCA basis requires re-executing the model-response pipeline with the original data/checkpoints. Absolute PC coordinates cannot be compared across those bases.
- The 31-city traffic archive includes negative coefficients (minimum approximately -0.0048245). Their values and feasibility flags are preserved. Stored fits are descriptive evidence, not exact convex replacement certificates. Missing historical pruning/tail curves, raw city prediction traces, and training caches were not recovered, and no curves were interpolated from images.
- Legacy BERT tables concern Transformer sentiment classifiers on SST-2, not modern-LLM evidence. Exact historical model revisions, original classifier predictions, and a fully pinned runtime were not recovered. The copied source and cached table transformations cannot establish bitwise full-inference reproduction.
- The original cross-domain `tables(1).zip`/`artifacts(1).zip` archives were not separately found on this server. The uploaded paper carries their byte-identity records and the required bytes, which were checked. The public ISQED source's two protocol blobs match the paper; this does not manufacture missing run-environment provenance.
- Cached LLM analysis replay regenerates tables from fitted split outcomes and archived bootstrap distributions. It does not perform fresh score-to-fit optimization or a new bootstrap. Full score-level entry points and frozen configurations are packaged, but that more expensive CPU campaign remains `not_run`.
- External inference design files are hash-manifested but not bundled. Model weights, dataset caches and legacy raw images are not copied. Availability of an upstream path is not a claim that every checkpoint/data version needed for full inference has been revalidated during packaging.
- Level-A numeric text outputs match the uploaded replay baseline byte-for-byte under the captured environment. PDFs are checked semantically and by layout; no cross-toolchain pixel-identity claim is made. Tectonic changed pagination in a probe; pdfLaTeX is the verified build engine.
- The source package and provenance contain identifying repository names and historical paths. No independently certified anonymous submission or blanket licensing grant is claimed.
